# Steady Prompter Privacy Policy

Steady Prompter does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

- No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, no servers.
- Your scripts are stored only on your device.
- The camera and microphone are used only while you record, and videos are saved only to your own Photos library.
- Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/j78fn4wwrs-bit/prompter
